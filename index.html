01Where value is lost

The process usually breaks before the people do.

When work is hard to see, measure, or repeat, cost quietly accumulates in hand-offs, errors, waiting, and excess stock.

System stock does not match reality

Stock-outs, emergency purchases, and long cycle counts erode margin and trust.

Work stalls between teams

Approvals and informal follow-ups stretch lead time while ownership stays unclear.

Critical work depends on memory

Spreadsheets and tribal knowledge make consistency, training, and handover fragile.

Problems surface too late

Delays and shortages become visible only after they affect dispatch or the customer.

02What I do

Practical services for the systems behind daily work.

The goal is not a presentation. It is a clearer process, workable controls, team adoption, and evidence that shows what changed.

Process Audit & Waste Diagnosis

I walk your floor, shadow your team and map how work really flows — then quantify the waste in time, cost and errors using Lean principles.

A prioritised list of fixes ranked by rupee impact

Workflow Mapping & Redesign

Current-state and future-state process maps, with bottlenecks, hand-offs and approvals removed or simplified.

Shorter lead times with fewer touches per order

Inventory & Warehouse Optimisation

Cycle counting, ABC classification, safety-stock levels, slotting and pick-path design to lift accuracy and cut carrying cost.

Accuracy up, stock-outs and dead stock down

Tracking Systems (QR + ERP)

Low-cost QR-based tracking for inventory and work-in-progress, integrated with your existing ERP, WMS or even Excel.

Real-time visibility without expensive software

SOPs & Standard Work

Clear, visual standard operating procedures and checklists so the process runs the same way every shift — with or without the expert.

Fewer errors, faster onboarding, less rework

KPI Dashboards & Governance

The handful of metrics that matter, reported simply, plus a review cadence so improvements stick and keep compounding.

Problems caught early, decisions made on data

Operational & Workflow Automation

Identify repetitive, rules-based tasks and streamline them with practical workflow automations — such as form-to-sheet updates, task and approval routing, status notifications, scheduled reports, and AI-assisted information handling.

Less manual follow-up, fewer repetitive steps, and clearer hand-offs

ProductionWIPFlow visibility

Work-in-progress tracking on the shop floor

Problem
Teams could not see where jobs were in production until dispatch dates began to slip.
Root cause
Stage hand-offs were not recorded, so queues and bottlenecks remained invisible.
Intervention
Mapped the workflow, installed QR checkpoints, and created simple reports for movement and queue time.

Live WIP status

Teams gained a shared view of job movement and a practical way to identify bottlenecks earlier.
