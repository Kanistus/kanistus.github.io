Operations · ERP · Tracking

End-to-End Inventory Tracking System Implementation

Problem
Inventory accuracy was at 85% — leading to stockout errors, fulfilment delays, and poor visibility into stock movement across the warehouse.
Approach
Designed and implemented a QR-based ERP-integrated tracking system to automate stock movement recording. Built KPI dashboards and forecasting reports for operational planning and replenishment decisions.
ERP System QR Tracking KPI Dashboards Inventory Forecasting
Outcome
Improved inventory accuracy from 85% to 97% — a 12-point gain — and enhanced workflow efficiency across procurement, warehouse, and dispatch operations.

Production · Real-Time Tracking

Work in Progress (WIP) Tracking System

Problem
Production floor had no real-time visibility into work-in-progress stages — causing undetected bottlenecks, delayed dispatches, and poor coordination between production and operations teams.
Approach
Designed and deployed a real-time QR-based WIP tracker integrated into the production workflow. Each stage was tagged and monitored, giving floor managers live status updates and bottleneck alerts.
QR Systems Process Mapping ERP Integration BPMN 2.0
Outcome
Improved production visibility significantly, reduced bottlenecks at key stages, and enabled data-driven decisions on scheduling and resource allocation.

The Bigger Picture

Long-Term Vision

"India's organic producers are fragmented. I want to build the infrastructure that connects them to the world — and prove that supply chain design is a form of social impact."

My entrepreneurial ambition goes beyond building profitable businesses. I am working toward a platform that unites organic farmers and organic product manufacturers across India into a single, trusted, and efficient marketplace.

The core insight is simple: India has extraordinary organic produce and artisan manufacturers, but they lack distribution infrastructure, market access, and quality trust signals. A purpose-built supply chain and marketplace platform can solve all three simultaneously.

Beyond this venture, I aspire to work in operations consulting — helping businesses at all stages design systems that are efficient, resilient, and built for scale. Whether it's a startup getting its first supply chain right, or an enterprise eliminating waste across a complex operation, I want to be the person who helps them see the system clearly.
